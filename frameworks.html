How we read the carbon and energy nexus

Announcements are cheap. These six frameworks are how we separate what is real from what is press release, and how we turn a headline into a decision for an investor, a regulator, an operator or a founder.

Electrons, molecules, pore space, water

Every story is sorted by where it lands. Electrons are power: grids, generation, data center load. Molecules are fuels and feedstocks: oil, gas, hydrogen, ammonia, biofuels. Pore space is the subsurface: CO2 storage, produced water, geothermal and the rights and liability that come with rock. Water runs through all three: it cools power plants, makes hydrogen, comes up with every barrel as produced water, and sits in the rock where CO2 is stored. Most money moves where two or more of these meet.

The nexus ripple

We trace each event outward in five steps: the trigger, the first market it moves, the second order effect, who pays or gains, and what it means for the reader. A rule in Brussels becomes a permit price, then a project's return, then a tax bill or a job.

The project ladder

Every tracked project sits on one of seven steps. Moving up a step needs public evidence.

  1. Announced
  2. Feasibility
  3. Engineering (FEED)
  4. Permits and contracts
  5. Final investment decision
  6. Construction
  7. Operating

Bankability

Five questions decide whether a carbon or energy project can borrow money: Is the revenue contracted? Is the policy support durable? Is the technology proven at this scale? Is the permit in hand? Is long term liability assigned? A project that cannot answer three of the five is a story, not an asset.

Policy durability

We rate support by how hard it is to undo: statute beats regulation, regulation beats guidance, and contracts beat all three. We also ask who would have to change their mind, and what it would cost them politically to do it.

Carbon integrity

For any claimed tonne we ask four things: Is it measured or modeled? Is it additional? How long is it stored? Who verifies it, and who is liable if it leaks? The answers set its fair price far more than the label on the credit.

Stages and ratings on this site are Carbon Energy Nexus judgment, built from public filings and reporting. They are not investment advice.

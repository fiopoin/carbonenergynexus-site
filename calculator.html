    The Nexus Score is a Carbon Energy Nexus method, not an official standard. It awards up to 20 points for each of five levers you control.

    How we calculate

    Each input is multiplied by a published emission factor. Electricity uses the US average rate for electricity consumed unless you enter your utility's rate. For companies, the clean share lowers a market-based Scope 2 estimate; the location-based figure is shown as well. Energy is converted with EIA heat contents. Flights and transit count toward emissions, not on-site energy.

    EPA says factors like these give approximate results and are not meant for formal emission inventories. For reporting, use your utility's factors, the GHG Protocol and the full EPA Emission Factors Hub.

    The Nexus Score levers

    Households: clean electricity share (up to 20), car efficiency, where an electric car or no car earns 20 and gas cars earn points from 20 mpg (0) to 50 mpg (20), heating (heat pump 20, electric resistance 10, fuel 0), flights under 10% of your footprint (20) or under 25% (10), and measured from real bills (20).

    Companies: clean electricity share (up to 20), electric share of the fleet (up to 20), measured from meters and invoices (20), an emissions target (internal 10, externally validated 20), and business flights under 10% of the footprint (20) or under 25% (10).

Carbon storage

Australia agrees to store Singapore's CO2 as Gorgon injected only 26% of its separated CO2 last year

A new treaty sets the rules for shipping captured CO2 across borders to sub-seabed storage. Western Australia's regulator says Gorgon's August plume was planned venting while its injection system was constrained.

Singapore and Australia signed a treaty in Canberra on Nov 24 that sets binding rules for shipping liquefied CO2 captured in Singapore to offshore geological storage under Australian jurisdiction, Carbon Herald reported Nov 25. Singapore has no domestic geological storage, and the agreement targets its refining, petrochemical and power sectors. Liability for fugitive emissions passes from Singapore to Australia at a defined handover point, such as a terminal, port or offshore buoy. A joint committee will meet at least once a year, and the treaty enters into force 30 days after both countries complete ratification.

The deal lands as Australia's largest CO2 injection project faces scrutiny. Western Australia's Department of Water and Environmental Regulation said a methane plume seen by satellite in August at Chevron's Gorgon LNG plant on Barrow Island was planned venting of reservoir CO2 through approved infrastructure during periods when capture and storage constraints limited operation, ABC News reported Nov 25. In the past financial year about 5.54 million tonnes of CO2 was separated from Gorgon gas and 1.45 million tonnes, or 26%, was injected. Five satellite observations averaged 4.9 tonnes of methane an hour, nearly five times the reported rate. The minister said the licence may be amended.

Pore space is also in demand for water. New Mexico is studying whether about 1.3 trillion gallons of brackish groundwater could be desalinated, with WSP planning two deep wells at Quail Ranch, and the state's brine plan names deep injection wells and evaporation ponds as disposal options, The Cool Down reported Nov 19. In Scotland, EnEarth agreed on Nov 23 to buy Storegga's 30% stake and lead role in the Acorn store, which targets more than 140 million tonnes of capacity.

Sources: Carbon Herald, Nov 25, 2026; ABC News, Nov 25, 2026; The Cool Down via Yahoo, Nov 19, 2026; Carbon Herald, Nov 23, 2026

Through the nexus

Electrons

Power is one of three Singapore sectors the treaty targets. With no storage at home, capture at its power plants depends on Australian pore space.

Molecules

Gorgon's LNG output has run about 8% above its 15.6 Mt a year design capacity since 2021, ABC reported, while only a quarter of its separated CO2 went underground last year.

Pore space

The handover point model splits liability between the sending and storing country. Acorn's two licensed North Sea stores sit about 2.5 km below the seabed.

Water

Brine from inland desalination competes for the same deep injection capacity. New Mexico's plan for 1.3 trillion gallons of brackish water lists injection wells as one disposal route.

Use it

Investors
Cross border storage opens Asian demand for Australian pore space. Watch ratification timing before pricing any shipping chain.
Regulators
Gorgon shows injection shortfalls turn into venting. Tie licences to measured injection rates and satellite checks.
Industry
Singapore emitters can now plan export chains. Define the handover point and its liability terms in every contract.
Founders
Monitoring is the gap. Independent measurement of injected and vented volumes is what regulators are now asking for.

The brief

Capital

Greek group EnEarth buys Storegga's 30% of Scotland's Acorn CO2 store and takes the lead role

EnEarth Holdings signed an agreement on Nov 23 to buy Storegga's 30% working interest and lead developer status in the Acorn CCS project in north east Scotland, subject to approvals and due to close by the end of 2026, Carbon Herald reported. Shell and Harbour Energy remain partners. Acorn's two licensed stores target 5 to 10 million tonnes a year of injection and more than 140 million tonnes of capacity. EnEarth says the deal creates an independent European CO2 transport and storage group spanning Acorn, Prinos in Greece, Devnya in Bulgaria and CO2 shipping.

Emitters
Scottish and shipped CO2 supply now has a new counterparty. Recheck terms with the incoming lead developer.
Investors
Independent storage platforms are consolidating across countries. Watch UK business case terms before close.

Sources: Carbon Herald, Nov 23, 2026; Scottish Financial Review, Nov 23, 2026

Projects

Europe builds hydrogen ready power and pipes: RWE wins 2.1 GW, Wood gets the BarMar subsea design

Germany's Federal Network Agency awarded RWE three hydrogen compatible gas plants in North Rhine Westphalia totaling about 2,100 MW in its long term capacity tender, and RWE said construction of the first should start within weeks, Renewable Energy Magazine reported Nov 23. On Nov 25, Wood won the front end engineering contract for BarMar, the roughly 380 km Barcelona to Marseille subsea section of H2med, designed for up to 2 million tonnes of hydrogen a year, with work running to April 2028.

Suppliers
Hydrogen ready turbines and 100% hydrogen compressors are now in live orders. Qualify early.
Developers
BarMar's timeline sets when Iberian hydrogen can reach France. Plan offtake around 2028 design completion.

Sources: Renewable Energy Magazine, Nov 23, 2026; Renewable Energy Magazine, Nov 25, 2026

Regulation

Senate bill heading to the House could make new data centers pay $55 billion for power lines

New data centers would pay about $55 billion in transmission charges over 10 years under the Senate passed Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act, about $35 billion more than without it, according to a Grid Strategies estimate reported by the Daily Caller on Nov 25. The Senate passed the bill 62 to 33 on Nov 19, and the House schedule lists it for the week of Nov 30. A Goldman Sachs note said US data center power capacity and demand have risen nearly 30% this year and that 52 GW more could come online in 2028.

Data center developers
Model higher transmission charges in site economics now, not after a FERC rule.
Utilities
FERC would have 90 days to act. Prepare cost allocation filings.

Sources: Daily Caller, Nov 25, 2026; Reuters via Business Standard, Nov 25, 2026

Water

UAE studies desalination plants on its east coast, outside the Gulf

The UAE is studying new desalination plants on its east coast and their links to transmission networks, and will expand underground water storage, Energy Minister Suhail Al Mazrouei said, The National reported Nov 26. The aim is to raise capacity and diversify where water is produced. In May, a 60 million imperial gallon a day reverse osmosis plant, Fujairah I, was signed at the Port of Fujairah on the Gulf of Oman for Dh1.046 billion ($284.8 million). The National noted that desalination plants in Bahrain and Kuwait were attacked in March.

Water investors
Site diversification is now a security goal. Expect more east coast tenders tied to grid links.
Power planners
Reverse osmosis load moves with the plants. Plan transmission to new water sites together.

Sources: The National, Nov 26, 2026

Projects

Italy's storage auction and a French geothermal heat network add firm low carbon capacity

A BW ESS and A2A joint venture won Italy's MACSE auction awards for four battery projects of about 400 MW and 2,608 MWh in Sicily and Brindisi, Renewable Energy Magazine reported Nov 25. In France, ENGIE signed a 30 year, €94 million contract on Nov 25 for a 36 km heat network in the Yvelines supplied by deep geothermal from the Dogger aquifer at about 1,500 m, delivering about 141 GWh of heat a year and avoiding about 27,700 tonnes of CO2, Energynews.pro reported.

Storage developers
Long duration auction contracts are bankable. Six and a half hour systems are winning in Italy.
Cities
Deep aquifer heat can serve whole districts. Construction on the Yvelines network starts in 2027.

Sources: Renewable Energy Magazine, Nov 25, 2026; Energynews.pro, Nov 26, 2026

Critical minerals and mining

Malaysia counts its rare earths as the price gap between China and the West widens, copper sits near its low for the year, and Canada and Finland test cleaner battery material supply.

NdRare earths

Malaysia reports 274,887 tonnes of surveyed rare earth resources

Minister Arthur Joseph Kurup told parliament on Nov 25 that surveys show 274,887 tonnes of rare earth resources as of 2025, with preliminary estimates of 16.1 million tonnes, and stressed these are not verified reserves. A RM38.49 million second phase covers 160 square km in 10 states. Lynas and South Korea's JS Link are building a 3,000 tonne a year magnet plant in Kuantan.

Sources: Malay Mail, Nov 25, 2026

YHeavy rare earths

Yttrium oxide costs about $1,650 a kg in North America against about $9 in China

The gap was recorded on Nov 17, Wisevoter reported Nov 25, citing The Northern Miner. China's controlled rare earth shipments to the US averaged 19.3 tonnes a month from November 2025 through September. Sprott analysts argue long term minimum price contracts can finance projects outside China.

Sources: Wisevoter, Nov 25, 2026

§Minerals policy

Price floors spread as the West tries to fund rare earth supply

MP Materials has a 10 year US Defense Department agreement with a $110 a kg floor for NdPr, and Lynas set up a price floor with JARE in March, Wisevoter reported Nov 25. China refined 91% of magnet rare earths in 2024 and made 94% of sintered magnets. Beijing's broader controls are suspended until Jan 10, 2027.

Sources: Wisevoter, Nov 25, 2026

CuCopper

LME copper slips to $11,738 near its lowest level this year

Three month copper fell 0.7% to $11,738 a tonne and tin fell 3.8% to $38,020, Wisevoter reported Nov 25, citing The Edge Malaysia. Copper hit a record near $15,000 in September. The CPER copper fund is down 19.39% over 20 sessions.

Sources: Wisevoter, Nov 25, 2026; Rio Times, Nov 25, 2026

CGraphite

Ontario's Kearney graphite mine tests a 22 tonne electric haul truck before restart

MEDATech Engineering is running a one week trial at the Kearney mine, funded by Natural Resources Canada, Global Battery Materials said Nov 23. The July study shows a US$183 million post tax NPV, about 23,000 tonnes a year of concentrate and a full restart in 2028. No binding offtake has been disclosed.

Sources: Discovery Alert, Nov 25, 2026

NiNickel and cobalt

Fortum makes first battery grade nickel and cobalt sulphate samples from recycled material in Finland

Fortum produced its first battery grade nickel sulphate, cobalt sulphate and lithium hydroxide samples at Harjavalta, SustainabilityOnline reported Nov 24. BASF agreed to sell its European and North American battery recycling business to cylib, with completion expected in mid 2027.

Sources: SustainabilityOnline, Nov 24, 2026
